Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Lotion Plus is a portfolio project, a notes application at https://lotionv2.netlify.app.

Google user data we access

If you sign in with Google, the app requests only the basic sign-in scopes: openid, email and profile. We receive your email address and basic profile information.

How we use it

Your email address is used only to identify you and to separate your notes from other users' notes. Your Google access token is used only to verify your identity with each request. We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail or any other Google service.

What we store

Your email address and the notes you create (title, content, timestamp) are stored in an Amazon DynamoDB table on AWS. Nothing else from your Google account is stored.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is processed only by the cloud services needed to run the app (AWS and Netlify).

Guest mode

Guest mode does not use Google sign-in. Guest notes are stored only in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers.

Deletion and contact

You can delete your notes inside the app at any time. To have your stored data removed entirely, email karadiaaryan@gmail.com and we will delete it.
